Same observations. Different calibration.

The classical attacker always assigns +1 to all nine observables. It fails only the final column, sampled with probability 1/12. The naive test assumes 1/6. The corrected test allows every setting probability to be as low as 1/12.

Why this threshold survives repeated checks

The corrected test multiplies by 57/55 after a win and 3/5 after a failure. If the conditional win probability is at most 11/12, the expected multiplier is at most 1. Lean checks that, on every valid finite adaptive tree, the probability of ever reaching evidence 100 is at most 1/100. Each tree can choose bets or stop using its past. This does not prove that a physical device obeys the model.

04 / WHAT ACTUALLY RAN

Moonlab

Native WASM gates and sequential Pauli measurements on two qubits. A 1% readout flip is applied independently to each recorded outcome.

QGTL

Fubini–Study metric and Berry-curvature kernels checked against analytic and numerical geometry, including gauge shifts.

quantum_rng

Seeded classical software mixer supplies native experiment draws, with rejection sampling for settings. No physical entropy claim.

Eshkol

Independent enumeration of all 512 value tables and six witnesses that attain the bias-aware bound.

Lean proves the abstract model and finite-tree theorem with real probabilities. Exact operator identities connect the ideal square to Pauli matrices. The simulator, compiler, random-number implementation and physical independence assumptions are outside that proof.
